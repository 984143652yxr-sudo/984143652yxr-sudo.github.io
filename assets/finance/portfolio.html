Methods and downloads

For each training target q, E minimizes historical CVaR; M minimizes variance. At an exactly fixed mean, minimizing normal ES also minimizes variance. Markowitz itself requires finite covariance, rather than normality. Curves join 25 fitted targets; green uses additional later-period targets and exact matched-mean solutions.
