Training H and N apply weights to historical one-day scenarios. Evaluation uses actual monthly holdings with daily weight drift. Window-average ES and whole-backtest ES are different summaries. Markowitz's default v = b / kα is a zero-mean normal scale conversion; its slider is independent.

Hindsight constraint and reproducibility

At α = 97.5%, a month with fewer than 40 trading days has empirical ES equal to its worst daily loss. For asset cumulative gross returns Gd, the oracle constraint is linear: [Gd − (1 − b/100)Gd−1]Tw ≥ 0 for every day. The solver therefore obtains the exact maximum monthly terminal wealth under this monthly ES constraint. O is a perfect-information monthly benchmark; training strategies never use future prices. Green's higher returns reflect future information as well as its terminal-wealth objective.

Returns come from the French ten-industry research portfolios. Cash earns 0%; costs, funding and execution frictions are excluded. Curves join 17 budget solutions; future paths need not be efficient frontiers.
